# Testing the new BUSS app – guide for colleagues

Thanks for helping test! This is a **test version** with fake students and startups. Please don't
enter real student details; make up names and use addresses like `test.student@buas.nl`.

You'll get from Shival: the link, your login (your first name in lower case) and the password.

- **Student form:** `<link>/register/`
- **Staff app:** `<link>/staff/`

Nothing is emailed: emails the app would send are only logged. Feel free to click everything; the
data is reset before each test round.

## As a student (10 minutes)

1. Fill in the form at `/register/` as an imaginary student. Try mistakes too: a 5-digit
   student number, a description over the word limit, skipping a required field.
2. Register again as a *graduation-track* student (study year "Year 4 and graduating within own
   company"), and once as a BUas employee.
3. Does the form read well? Is anything unclear or missing compared with the Microsoft Form?

## As a coach (20 minutes)

1. **Intake queue**: find the student you just registered. Does the deadline (10 working days)
   look right? Schedule the intake, then mark it as held.
2. **Pipeline**: drag a startup to another stage.
3. **Startups / Students**: open one, add a note to the activity log and a follow-up for yourself.
   Check *Follow-ups* afterwards.
4. **Dashboard**: do the figures make sense? Which would you actually use?
5. **Export**: export your caseload to Excel.

## Admins only

- *Admin ▾ → Team & roles*, *Import*, *Privacy (GDPR)* and *Configuration* (form texts, email
  templates, coaches, closure days).

## Feedback

For each finding, note **what you did, what you expected and what happened**, plus a screenshot
if possible, and send it to Shival. Most useful:

1. anything that blocks your normal way of working;
2. things that are confusing or take too many clicks;
3. missing fields or wrong wording;
4. nice-to-haves.
